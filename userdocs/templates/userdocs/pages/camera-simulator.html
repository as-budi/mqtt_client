Simulate a camera (ESP32-CAM) in the ESP32-CAM card (shown for IoT Platform devices) makes this page act like the ESP32-CAM template of IoT Platform. It is switched on automatically when the device's board on IoT Platform is ESP32-CAM, and works for any device you connect as.

How it works

  • A capture command (from Capture now on IoT Platform, or a rule's Capture snapshot action) is answered with a picture: the page draws it, uploads it, then replies {"success": true, "snapshot": <id>, "size": <bytes>}.
  • Pictures are not sent over MQTT (the broker accepts small messages only). The page uploads them to this server, which forwards them to IoT Platform's snapshot address for the device, exactly like the ESP32-CAM does over HTTP.
  • Every N s takes a picture regularly (0 = only on request, at least 2 s). It is also set by the requested state snapshot_interval.
  • Capture & upload now sends one picture right away, as if a button on the device were pressed.
  • The last picture, the result of the upload and the previous results are shown in the panel, with a link to the device's snapshots on IoT Platform.

Picture sources

Test patterna coloured pattern with the time, the device name and the command number

Webcamyour computer's camera. The browser asks for permission; the camera stays on only while this source is chosen

Picture settings

Like on the ESP32-CAM, the requested states framesize (size: 5 = QVGA 320×240, 8 = VGA 640×480, 9 = SVGA 800×600 … 13 = UXGA 1600×1200), quality (10 = best … 63 = smallest) and flash (brighter picture) are used for the next pictures and reported back. Pictures larger than about 500 KB are compressed more until they fit.
