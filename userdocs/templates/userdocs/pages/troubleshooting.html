The reason a connection failed is shown above the connection page. The most common ones:

The connection does not open

MessageWhat to check
Could not open wss://…host, port and WebSocket path (/mqtt or /); whether the broker has a WebSocket port at all. Without one, use mqtt:// or mqtts:// (relay)
Wrong username or passwordthe password typed on the page; for device connections, whether the device is still active on IoT Platform
Not authorizedthe broker refuses this login, e.g. the IoT Platform broker with a deactivated device
Nothing happens on an https page with ws://browsers block unencrypted WebSockets from secure pages; use wss://
Reconnecting in N sthe broker or the network is unreachable for now; the page keeps trying

Subscriptions and messages

  • refused next to a subscription: the broker does not allow that topic for your login. A device on IoT Platform may only use its own topics; a sandbox account only sandbox/<you>/# and sandbox/public/#.
  • Messages do not appear: check that a subscription matches the topic exactly (case matters), that it is not muted, that Pause is off and that the filter is empty.
  • An old value keeps coming back: it is a retained message. Remove it with Clear retained.
  • A device connection kicks out the real device: an IoT Platform device and this page may be connected at the same time, but rules and dashboards see both as the same device. Disconnect one of them when testing.

Signing in and IoT Platform

  • Your IoT Platform session has ended: sign in again. This happens after you sign out of IoT Platform, revoke this app under Connected apps, or your account is deactivated.
  • Sign in again to allow: a newer feature needs an extra permission; sign out and sign in again, then choose Allow.
  • IoT Platform cannot be reached: the platform is temporarily down. Connections to other brokers keep working.
  • You need Editor access to this device: ask the owner to share the device with you as Editor.

Camera simulator

  • Webcam: access was refused: allow the camera for this site via the icon in the browser's address bar, then choose the source again.
  • the server forbids the camera: this server's configuration blocks the camera; ask the administrator.
  • the webcam needs HTTPS: open the app over https:// (or localhost).
  • 413 / 429 from IoT Platform: the picture is too large (lower framesize or quality) or sent too often.
