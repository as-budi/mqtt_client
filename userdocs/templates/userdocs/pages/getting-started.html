The MQTT Client is a web app for testing MQTT from your browser. It belongs to IoT Platform: you sign in with your IoT Platform account, and you can connect as one of your devices to try dashboards, rules and commands without an ESP32. Nothing needs to be installed.

What you can do

  • Connect to any MQTT broker: brokers with a WebSocket port (wss://, ws://) directly from the browser, and ordinary brokers (mqtt:// port 1883, mqtts:// port 8883) through a relay on this server.
  • Subscribe to topics, publish messages, and read them in a list, a topic tree or small charts.
  • Keep connection profiles, subscriptions and frequently used messages in your account.
  • Behave like an IoT Platform device: send simulated sensor data, answer commands, report requested states, simulate firmware updates and camera pictures.

Your first connection

  1. Sign in on the home page with Sign in with IoT Platform. The first time, IoT Platform asks you to allow this app to read your devices (see Your account).
  2. On Connections, open New connection and choose a ready-made broker, for example EMQX public broker, then Create.
  3. Open the connection and press Connect. The status turns Connected.
  4. Under Subscriptions, subscribe to a topic of your own, e.g. filkom/yourname/#.
  5. Under Publish, send {"temperature": 25.1} to filkom/yourname/room. The message appears twice on the right: once going out (↑) and once coming back from the broker (↓).

Public brokers are open to everyone: anyone can read and write any topic. Use a topic name nobody else is likely to use, and never send passwords or personal data there.

Working with IoT Platform

To test a device of yours, go to Connections → From IoT Platform and choose Create connection next to the device. The new profile logs in as that device, with its topics already filled in. The data you publish appears on IoT Platform exactly as if the real device had sent it. See IoT Platform devices and sandbox and Behaving like a device.

Where your data lives

WhatKept
Connection profiles, saved subscriptions, saved messages, stylein your account on this server
Messages you send and receiveonly in the open browser tab; gone when you close it (use Export to keep them)
Broker passwordsnever on the server; typed in the page, or remembered in this browser if you tick Remember here
Device access tokensnever stored; fetched from IoT Platform each time you connect
