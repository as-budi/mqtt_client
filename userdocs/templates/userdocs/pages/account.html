The MQTT Client has no accounts or passwords of its own. You sign in with your IoT Platform account; IoT Platform checks your password, and this app never sees it.

Signing in

  1. Press Sign in with IoT Platform on the home page. You go to IoT Platform's login page (or straight back, if you are already logged in there).
  2. The first time, IoT Platform shows what this app may do: read your name and email, list your devices (and, for devices you may edit, their access tokens), and use your MQTT sandbox account. Choose Allow.
  3. You come back to Connections.

No account yet? Use Create an account: it opens IoT Platform's sign-up page, and you come back here after activating the account.

After a new feature is added, IoT Platform may ask again for a new permission (for example for the MQTT sandbox). When a page says Sign in again to allow, sign out and sign in again.

Signing out

  • Sign out (account menu, top right) ends your session in this app.
  • Sign out of IoT Platform too also logs you out of IoT Platform in this browser.
  • To remove this app's access completely, go to IoT Platform → Profile → Connected apps and revoke it. You are then signed out here as well within about ten minutes.
