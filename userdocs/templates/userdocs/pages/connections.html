A connection profile keeps everything needed to reach one broker. Profiles are listed on Connections, with Open, Edit, Duplicate and Delete.

Each profile has a name (unique in your account) and a colour, shown as a stripe on its card and page.

Host is only the name or IP address (no wss:// in front). WebSocket path (only for ws and wss) is usually /mqtt; some brokers use /. An empty port means the default of the protocol.

A page served over https:// may not open ws:// addresses on the internet: browsers block unencrypted WebSockets from secure pages. Use wss://, or mqtt:// through the relay.

Direct and Relay

A browser can only open WebSocket connections. Direct profiles (ws, wss) connect from your browser straight to the broker. Relay profiles (mqtt, mqtts) go through the relay on this server, which only passes the bytes on; MQTT itself still runs in your browser, so every feature works the same.

Saved subscriptions

A profile can keep topic filters with a QoS and a colour. They are subscribed automatically on every connect. A subscription can be muted: it stays subscribed but its messages are hidden from the list. Change them in the profile form, or add and remove them on the connection page for that visit only.

Certificates

  • mqtts://: the relay checks the broker's certificate against the usual public authorities. For a broker with a self-signed certificate or a campus CA, paste the CA certificate (PEM, starting with -----BEGIN CERTIFICATE-----) in the profile. Never paste a private key; the form refuses it.
  • wss://: your browser checks the certificate. For a self-signed broker, open https://host:port once and accept the certificate, or install the CA on your computer.
